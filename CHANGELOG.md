## 1.0.0

* Count rebuilds of widgets wrapped in `RebuildInspector`, including parent-driven builds and child `setState`.
* Flash a widget's bounds when it rebuilds, without counting the flash itself.
* Show an in-app overlay with frequency bands, a timeline, and Markdown or JSON export.
* Compare a marked baseline with later counts.
* Optionally detect other user-widget rebuilds in debug mode.
